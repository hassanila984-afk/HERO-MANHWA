# Manhwati — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

Manhwati is a personal manhwa reading app for Android. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

## Reading without an account

You can read, translate and browse without signing in. Your library, reading history, settings and downloads stay on your phone. If you turn on Google Drive sync, they are saved to a file in your own Google Drive.

To show the app owner how many people are using the app, the app sends an anonymous activity signal about once a minute while it is open. The signal contains the screen you are on and a random device ID. It does not contain your name or email. Turning on Incognito mode in the app stops this signal.

## Signing in with Google

Signing in is only needed to write in the public chat and to have a profile. When you sign in, the app receives your Google account's:

- **email address**, which is never shown to other users;
- **name** and **profile picture**, which you can change.

The app does not ask for any other Google permissions. It cannot read your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else.

## What signed-in users share

- **Profile:** your display name, profile picture, short bio and up to three favorite titles. Other users of the app can see these.
- **Chat messages:** messages you post in the public chat. All app users can see them, and the admin can delete them.
- **Activity:** whether you are online, the screen you have open (including the title you are reading), and how long you use the app each day. Only the app admin can see this.

## Translation and the "System" assistant

When you translate a chapter or chat with the in-app assistant, the text is sent to Google Gemini through the app's server so a reply can be generated. Text from the assistant chat is not shown to other users. The assistant's memory of your preferences is stored on your phone, and you can view or delete it in the app.

## Where data is stored

Account, chat and activity data is stored on the app's server (Supabase). Data is never sold or shared with advertisers. The app shows no ads.
